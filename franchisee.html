Franchisee

Created on demand. Whether and how a Franchisee is created depends on the merchant's franchise business model. The create-form fields are the same in all cases.

⚠️ Franchisee creation depends on the business model. Foodics supports two franchise models, and they differ in who owns the licence renewal. Pick the model that matches the merchant before creating Franchisees.
Model A — Franchisor-owned

Franchisor owns the account & distributes licences

The franchisor (the brand owner) owns the Foodics account and distributes licences to its franchisees from within that account.

🔁 Renewal ownership: Franchisor — the brand owner is responsible for renewing those licences.
Model B — Franchisee-owned (merged)

Franchisee buys Foodics & merges into the main account

The franchisor asks the franchisee to buy Foodics and merge their account into the franchisor's main account.

🔁 Renewal ownership: Franchisee — each franchisee is responsible for renewing its own licences.
